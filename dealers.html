Questions

Good questions before you sign up.

Where do the grades come from?

From the complaints owners file with NHTSA, the federal agency that tracks vehicle defects and runs recalls. We've sorted more than 800,000 of them by problem and compared each model year with other cars from the same year, adjusted for how many were sold.

Does it tell me if this specific car will break?

No. Grades describe the model year, not one car's condition. They tell you what to look for and how much risk you're taking on, so you know which condition reports and inspections matter most.

Which cars are covered?

121 popular models from 2000 to 2024, including the trucks, SUVs and sedans that make up most auction lanes. Brand-new model years show up once there's enough owner data to grade them fairly.

Does it work at the auction?

Yes. It runs in your phone's browser. Scan the VIN barcode on the car or the auction label, add the miles, and the car is graded in seconds.

How does the free trial work?

You get 14 days free. You add a card at sign-up and aren't charged until the trial ends. Cancel anytime from Plan & billing, and you won't be charged.
