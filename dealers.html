How it works

1. Add your run listPaste VINs with miles, one per line, or add cars one at a time. VINs are decoded with NHTSA's free decoder.
2. See the grade and the riskEach car shows its model year's grade, its most-reported problems, and where its mileage sits against the mileages owners report those failures at.
3. Bid on the right carsCars are sorted into Bid, Caution and Pass so you can skip the ones most likely to come back as recon headaches.
