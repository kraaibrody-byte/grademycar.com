Chevrolet Tahoe reliability FAQ

Is the Chevrolet Tahoe reliable?

Average reliability. The Chevrolet Tahoe earns a B- overall reliability grade from GradeMyCar, with a B for its engine and a C+ for its transmission. It ranks 2nd of 6 full-size SUVs we grade. The transmission (C+) is the weaker of its two major components, so ask for transmission service records and pay close attention to shifting on a test drive. Grades are based on 2,553 owner complaints filed with NHTSA for the 2013-2023 model years, adjusted for how many were sold.

How many miles will a Chevrolet Tahoe last?

A well-maintained Chevrolet Tahoe typically lasts around 270,000 miles, and owners have reported examples reaching 380,000 miles. About 19.7% reach 250,000 miles.

What are the best years for the Chevrolet Tahoe?

Based on NHTSA owner complaints, the most reliable Chevrolet Tahoe model years are 2006, 2010, 2012, 2013 and 2014.

What Chevrolet Tahoe years should I avoid?

Based on NHTSA owner complaints, the Chevrolet Tahoe years to be most careful with are 2021, 2022 and 2023. The 2021 grades an F overall (engine F, transmission D), and its top engine/transmission problem is stalling / loss of power, which a recall covers. The 2023 grades an F overall (engine F, transmission F), and its top engine/transmission problem is stalling / loss of power.

Is the Chevrolet Tahoe transmission reliable?

GradeMyCar grades the Chevrolet Tahoe transmission a C+ (average reliability). Reliability varies by model year, so grade the specific year you're looking at.

Is the Chevrolet Tahoe engine reliable?

GradeMyCar grades the Chevrolet Tahoe engine a B (above-average reliability).

Grades are estimates for a make and model across model years, not a specific vehicle. Always get a pre-purchase inspection and check open recalls by VIN.
