Ford Fiesta reliability FAQ

Is the Ford Fiesta reliable?

Poor reliability. The Ford Fiesta earns a D overall reliability grade from GradeMyCar, with a C+ for its engine and an F for its transmission. It ranks 13th of 14 compact cars we grade. The transmission (F) is the weaker of its two major components, so ask for transmission service records and pay close attention to shifting on a test drive. Grades are based on 1,819 owner complaints filed with NHTSA for the 2013-2019 model years, adjusted for how many were sold.

How many miles will a Ford Fiesta last?

A well-maintained Ford Fiesta typically lasts around 140,000 miles, and owners have reported examples reaching 190,000 miles. About 6.8% reach 250,000 miles.

What are the best years for the Ford Fiesta?

No Ford Fiesta model year stands out as exceptionally reliable in NHTSA owner complaints. The best-graded years are 2016, 2017 and 2018.

What Ford Fiesta years should I avoid?

Based on NHTSA owner complaints, the Ford Fiesta years to be most careful with are 2011, 2012, 2013, 2014 and 2015. The 2011 grades an F overall (engine D, transmission F). The 2012 grades an F overall (engine C, transmission F).

Is the Ford Fiesta transmission reliable?

GradeMyCar grades the Ford Fiesta transmission an F (poor reliability). Reliability varies by model year, so grade the specific year you're looking at.

Is the Ford Fiesta engine reliable?

GradeMyCar grades the Ford Fiesta engine a C+ (average reliability).
