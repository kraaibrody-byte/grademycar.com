Volkswagen Golf reliability FAQ

Is the Volkswagen Golf reliable?

Average reliability. The Volkswagen Golf earns a B- overall reliability grade from GradeMyCar, with a B for its engine and a B- for its transmission. It ranks 8th of 11 compact cars we grade. Its engine (B) and transmission (B-) grade about the same, so neither stands out as a weak point.

How many miles will a Volkswagen Golf last?

A well-maintained Volkswagen Golf typically lasts around 185,000 miles, and owners have reported examples reaching 210,000 miles. About 7.5% reach 250,000 miles.

Is the Volkswagen Golf transmission reliable?

GradeMyCar grades the Volkswagen Golf transmission a B- (average reliability). Reliability varies by model year, so grade the specific year you're looking at.

Is the Volkswagen Golf engine reliable?

GradeMyCar grades the Volkswagen Golf engine a B (above-average reliability).
