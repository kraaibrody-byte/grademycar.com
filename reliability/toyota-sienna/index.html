How many miles will a Toyota Sienna last?

A well-maintained Toyota Sienna typically lasts around 180,000 miles, and owners have reported examples reaching 250,000 miles. About 8.9% reach 250,000 miles.

Based on NHTSA owner complaints, the most reliable Toyota Sienna model years are 2014, 2016, 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022 and 2023.
