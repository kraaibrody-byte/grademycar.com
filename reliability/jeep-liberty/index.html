How many miles will a Jeep Liberty last?

We estimate a well-maintained Jeep Liberty lasts around 160,000 miles. That's a rough figure for the model across all years, not measured for each model year, so check the grade for the year you're looking at.

What are the best years for the Jeep Liberty?

No Jeep Liberty model year stands out as exceptionally reliable in NHTSA owner complaints. The best-graded years are 2009, 2010 and 2011.

What Jeep Liberty years should I avoid?

Based on NHTSA owner complaints, the Jeep Liberty years to be most careful with are 2002, 2003, 2005, 2006, 2007 and 2008. The 2002 grades a D overall (engine D, transmission C), and its top engine/transmission problem is stalling / loss of power, which a recall covers.
