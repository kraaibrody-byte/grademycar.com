Subaru Ascent reliability FAQ

Is the Subaru Ascent reliable?

Average reliability. The Subaru Ascent earns a C+ overall reliability grade from GradeMyCar, with a B for its engine and a C- for its transmission. It ranks 10th of 23 midsize SUVs we grade. The transmission (C-) is the weaker of its two major components, so ask for transmission service records and pay close attention to shifting on a test drive. Grades are based on 965 owner complaints filed with NHTSA for the 2019-2023 model years, adjusted for how many were sold.

How many miles will a Subaru Ascent last?

A well-maintained Subaru Ascent typically lasts around 170,000 miles, and owners have reported examples reaching 240,000 miles. About 8.4% reach 250,000 miles.

What are the best years for the Subaru Ascent?

No Subaru Ascent model year stands out as exceptionally reliable in NHTSA owner complaints. The best-graded years are 2021, 2022 and 2023.

What Subaru Ascent years should I avoid?

Based on NHTSA owner complaints, the Subaru Ascent years to be most careful with are 2019 and 2020. The 2019 grades a D overall (engine C+, transmission D). The 2020 grades a D overall (engine C+, transmission D).

Is the Subaru Ascent transmission reliable?

GradeMyCar grades the Subaru Ascent transmission a C- (below-average reliability). Reliability varies by model year, so grade the specific year you're looking at.

Is the Subaru Ascent engine reliable?

GradeMyCar grades the Subaru Ascent engine a B (above-average reliability).
