GMC Terrain reliability FAQ

How many miles will a GMC Terrain last?

A well-maintained GMC Terrain typically lasts around 140,000 miles, and owners have reported examples reaching 200,000 miles. About 6.8% reach 250,000 miles.

What are the best years for the GMC Terrain?

No GMC Terrain model year stands out as exceptionally reliable in NHTSA owner complaints. The best-graded years are 2016, 2017 and 2021.

What GMC Terrain years should I avoid?

Based on NHTSA owner complaints, the GMC Terrain years to be most careful with are 2011. The 2011 grades a D overall (engine D, transmission C-).
