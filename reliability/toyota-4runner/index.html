Toyota 4Runner reliability FAQ

Is the Toyota 4Runner reliable?

Excellent reliability. The Toyota 4Runner earns an A overall reliability grade from GradeMyCar, with an A for its engine and an A for its transmission. It ranks 1st of 22 midsize SUVs we grade. Its engine (A) and transmission (A) grade about the same, so neither stands out as a weak point.

How many miles will a Toyota 4Runner last?

A well-maintained Toyota 4Runner typically lasts around 320,000 miles, and owners have reported examples reaching 450,000 miles. About 26.8% reach 250,000 miles.

GradeMyCar grades the Toyota 4Runner transmission an A (excellent reliability). Reliability varies by model year, so grade the specific year you're looking at.
