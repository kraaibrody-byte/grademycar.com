Chevrolet Equinox reliability FAQ

Is the Chevrolet Equinox reliable?

Above-average reliability. The Chevrolet Equinox earns a B overall reliability grade from GradeMyCar, with a C+ for its engine and a B+ for its transmission. It ranks 5th of 13 compact SUVs we grade. The engine (C+) is the weaker of its two major components, so ask for oil-change records and have a mechanic check for leaks and oil consumption. Grades are based on 4,040 owner complaints filed with NHTSA for the 2013-2023 model years, adjusted for how many were sold.

How many miles will a Chevrolet Equinox last?

A well-maintained Chevrolet Equinox typically lasts around 160,000 miles, and owners have reported examples reaching 200,000 miles. About 6.9% reach 250,000 miles.

What are the best years for the Chevrolet Equinox?

No Chevrolet Equinox model year stands out as exceptionally reliable in NHTSA owner complaints. The best-graded years are 2009, 2016 and 2017.

What Chevrolet Equinox years should I avoid?

Based on NHTSA owner complaints, the Chevrolet Equinox years to be most careful with are 2005 and 2010. The 2005 grades a D overall (engine D, transmission D), and its top engine/transmission problem is head gasket / coolant / overheating. The 2010 grades a D overall (engine C-, transmission F), and its top engine/transmission problem is stalling / loss of power.

Is the Chevrolet Equinox transmission reliable?

GradeMyCar grades the Chevrolet Equinox transmission a B+ (above-average reliability). Reliability varies by model year, so grade the specific year you're looking at.

Is the Chevrolet Equinox engine reliable?

GradeMyCar grades the Chevrolet Equinox engine a C+ (average reliability).
