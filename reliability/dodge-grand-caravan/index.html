Dodge Grand Caravan reliability FAQ

Is the Dodge Grand Caravan reliable?

Above-average reliability. The Dodge Grand Caravan earns a B overall reliability grade from GradeMyCar, with a B+ for its engine and a B- for its transmission. It ranks 2nd of 5 minivans we grade. The transmission (B-) is the weaker of its two major components, so ask for transmission service records and pay close attention to shifting on a test drive. Grades are based on 3,019 owner complaints filed with NHTSA for the 2013-2020 model years, adjusted for how many were sold.

How many miles will a Dodge Grand Caravan last?

A well-maintained Dodge Grand Caravan typically lasts around 150,000 miles, and owners have reported examples reaching 210,000 miles. About 7.2% reach 250,000 miles.

What are the best years for the Dodge Grand Caravan?

Based on NHTSA owner complaints, the most reliable Dodge Grand Caravan model years are 2019 and 2020.

What Dodge Grand Caravan years should I avoid?

Based on NHTSA owner complaints, the Dodge Grand Caravan years to be most careful with are 2008 and 2016. The 2008 grades a D overall (engine C-, transmission C-), and its top engine/transmission problem is stalling / loss of power. The 2016 grades a D overall (engine C, transmission F), and its top engine/transmission problem is transmission slipping / rough shifting, which a recall covers.

Is the Dodge Grand Caravan transmission reliable?

GradeMyCar grades the Dodge Grand Caravan transmission a B- (average reliability). Reliability varies by model year, so grade the specific year you're looking at.

Is the Dodge Grand Caravan engine reliable?

GradeMyCar grades the Dodge Grand Caravan engine a B+ (above-average reliability).
