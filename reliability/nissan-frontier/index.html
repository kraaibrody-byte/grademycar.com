Nissan Frontier reliability FAQ

Is the Nissan Frontier reliable?

Excellent reliability. The Nissan Frontier earns an A overall reliability grade from GradeMyCar, with an A for its engine and an A- for its transmission. It ranks 1st of 6 midsize trucks we grade. Its engine (A) and transmission (A-) grade about the same, so neither stands out as a weak point. Grades are based on 477 owner complaints filed with NHTSA for the 2013-2023 model years, adjusted for how many were sold.

How many miles will a Nissan Frontier last?

A well-maintained Nissan Frontier typically lasts around 180,000 miles, and owners have reported examples reaching 260,000 miles. About 8.9% reach 250,000 miles.

What are the best years for the Nissan Frontier?

Based on NHTSA owner complaints, the most reliable Nissan Frontier model years are 2011, 2013, 2014, 2015, 2016, 2017, 2018, 2019 and 2021.

What Nissan Frontier years should I avoid?

Based on NHTSA owner complaints, the Nissan Frontier years to be most careful with are 2005, 2006 and 2007. The 2005 grades an F overall (engine D, transmission F), and its most common complaint is transmission slipping / rough shifting. The 2006 grades an F overall (engine D, transmission F), and its most common complaint is transmission slipping / rough shifting.

Is the Nissan Frontier transmission reliable?

GradeMyCar grades the Nissan Frontier transmission an A- (excellent reliability). Reliability varies by model year, so grade the specific year you're looking at.

Is the Nissan Frontier engine reliable?

GradeMyCar grades the Nissan Frontier engine an A (excellent reliability).
