Toyota Highlander reliability FAQ

Is the Toyota Highlander reliable?

Above-average reliability. The Toyota Highlander earns a B+ overall reliability grade from GradeMyCar, with an A- for its engine and a B for its transmission. It ranks 4th of 23 midsize SUVs we grade. The transmission (B) is the weaker of its two major components, so ask for transmission service records and pay close attention to shifting on a test drive. Grades are based on 3,031 owner complaints filed with NHTSA for the 2013-2023 model years, adjusted for how many were sold.

How many miles will a Toyota Highlander last?

A well-maintained Toyota Highlander typically lasts around 250,000 miles, and owners have reported examples reaching 350,000 miles. About 17.1% reach 250,000 miles.

What are the best years for the Toyota Highlander?

Based on NHTSA owner complaints, the most reliable Toyota Highlander model years are 2012, 2014, 2015 and 2016.

What Toyota Highlander years should I avoid?

No Toyota Highlander model year stands out as a problem year in NHTSA owner complaints.

Is the Toyota Highlander transmission reliable?

Is the Toyota Highlander engine reliable?

GradeMyCar grades the Toyota Highlander engine an A- (excellent reliability).
