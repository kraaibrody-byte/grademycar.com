Volkswagen Tiguan reliability FAQ

Is the Volkswagen Tiguan reliable?

Above-average reliability. The Volkswagen Tiguan earns a B overall reliability grade from GradeMyCar, with a B- for its engine and a B+ for its transmission. It ranks 5th of 13 compact SUVs we grade. The engine (B-) is the weaker of its two major components, so ask for oil-change records and have a mechanic check for leaks and oil consumption. Grades are based on 1,557 owner complaints filed with NHTSA for the 2013-2023 model years, adjusted for how many were sold.

How many miles will a Volkswagen Tiguan last?

A well-maintained Volkswagen Tiguan typically lasts around 175,000 miles, and owners have reported examples reaching 200,000 miles. About 7.2% reach 250,000 miles.

What are the best years for the Volkswagen Tiguan?

Based on NHTSA owner complaints, the most reliable Volkswagen Tiguan model years are 2014, 2015, 2016 and 2017.

What Volkswagen Tiguan years should I avoid?

Based on NHTSA owner complaints, the Volkswagen Tiguan years to be most careful with are 2018 and 2022. The 2022 grades an F overall (engine F, transmission D). The 2018 grades a D overall (engine C-, transmission C-).

Is the Volkswagen Tiguan transmission reliable?

GradeMyCar grades the Volkswagen Tiguan transmission a B+ (above-average reliability). Reliability varies by model year, so grade the specific year you're looking at.

Is the Volkswagen Tiguan engine reliable?

GradeMyCar grades the Volkswagen Tiguan engine a B- (average reliability).

Grades are estimates for a make and model across model years, not a specific vehicle. Always get a pre-purchase inspection and check open recalls by VIN.
