Dodge Journey reliability FAQ

Is the Dodge Journey reliable?

Average reliability. The Dodge Journey earns a B- overall reliability grade from GradeMyCar, with a B- for its engine and a B- for its transmission. It ranks 7th of 23 midsize SUVs we grade. Its engine (B-) and transmission (B-) grade about the same, so neither stands out as a weak point. Grades are based on 2,310 owner complaints filed with NHTSA for the 2013-2020 model years, adjusted for how many were sold.

How many miles will a Dodge Journey last?

A well-maintained Dodge Journey typically lasts around 140,000 miles, and owners have reported examples reaching 190,000 miles. About 6.7% reach 250,000 miles.

What are the best years for the Dodge Journey?

Based on NHTSA owner complaints, the most reliable Dodge Journey model years are 2019 and 2020.

What Dodge Journey years should I avoid?

Based on NHTSA owner complaints, the Dodge Journey years to be most careful with are 2009, 2010, 2015 and 2016. The 2009 grades an F overall (engine F, transmission F). The 2010 grades an F overall (engine D, transmission C-).

Is the Dodge Journey transmission reliable?

GradeMyCar grades the Dodge Journey transmission a B- (average reliability). Reliability varies by model year, so grade the specific year you're looking at.

Is the Dodge Journey engine reliable?
