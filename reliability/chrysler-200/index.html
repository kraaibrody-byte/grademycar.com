Chrysler 200 reliability FAQ

Is the Chrysler 200 reliable?

Average reliability. The Chrysler 200 earns a B- overall reliability grade from GradeMyCar, with a C+ for its engine and a C+ for its transmission. It ranks 7th of 11 midsize cars we grade. Its engine (C+) and transmission (C+) grade about the same, so neither stands out as a weak point. How many miles will a Chrysler 200 last?

A well-maintained Chrysler 200 typically lasts around 140,000 miles, and owners have reported examples reaching 190,000 miles. About 6.6% reach 250,000 miles.

What are the best years for the Chrysler 200?

Based on NHTSA owner complaints, the most reliable Chrysler 200 model years are 2017.

What Chrysler 200 years should I avoid?

Based on NHTSA owner complaints, the Chrysler 200 years to be most careful with are 2015. The 2015 grades an F overall (engine F, transmission F), and its top engine/transmission problem is stalling / loss of power, which a recall covers.
