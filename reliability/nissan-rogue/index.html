Nissan Rogue reliability FAQ

Is the Nissan Rogue reliable?

Above-average reliability. The Nissan Rogue earns a B+ overall reliability grade from GradeMyCar, with a B+ for its engine and a B- for its transmission. It ranks 2nd of 13 compact SUVs we grade. The transmission (B-) is the weaker of its two major components, so ask for transmission service records and pay close attention to shifting on a test drive. Grades are based on 5,125 owner complaints filed with NHTSA for the 2013-2023 model years, adjusted for how many were sold.

How many miles will a Nissan Rogue last?

A well-maintained Nissan Rogue typically lasts around 150,000 miles, and owners have reported examples reaching 210,000 miles. About 7.3% reach 250,000 miles.

What are the best years for the Nissan Rogue?

Based on NHTSA owner complaints, the most reliable Nissan Rogue model years are 2018, 2019 and 2020.

What Nissan Rogue years should I avoid?

Based on NHTSA owner complaints, the Nissan Rogue years to be most careful with are 2023. The 2023 grades a D overall (engine F, transmission D), and its top engine/transmission problem is stalling / loss of power.

Is the Nissan Rogue transmission reliable?

GradeMyCar grades the Nissan Rogue transmission a B- (average reliability). Reliability varies by model year, so grade the specific year you're looking at.

Is the Nissan Rogue engine reliable?
