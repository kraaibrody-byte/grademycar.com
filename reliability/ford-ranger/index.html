Ford Ranger reliability FAQ

Is the Ford Ranger reliable?

Average reliability. The Ford Ranger earns a B- overall reliability grade from GradeMyCar, with a B+ for its engine and a D for its transmission. It ranks 3rd of 6 midsize trucks we grade. The transmission (D) is the weaker of its two major components, so ask for transmission service records and pay close attention to shifting on a test drive. Grades are based on 569 owner complaints filed with NHTSA for the 2019-2023 model years, adjusted for how many were sold.

How many miles will a Ford Ranger last?

A well-maintained Ford Ranger typically lasts around 190,000 miles, and owners have reported examples reaching 280,000 miles. About 9.8% reach 250,000 miles.

What are the best years for the Ford Ranger?

Based on NHTSA owner complaints, the most reliable Ford Ranger model years are 2007, 2008, 2009, 2010 and 2011.

What Ford Ranger years should I avoid?

No Ford Ranger model year stands out as a problem year in NHTSA owner complaints.

Is the Ford Ranger transmission reliable?

GradeMyCar grades the Ford Ranger transmission a D (poor reliability). Reliability varies by model year, so grade the specific year you're looking at.

Is the Ford Ranger engine reliable?

GradeMyCar grades the Ford Ranger engine a B+ (above-average reliability).
