Mazda CX-9 reliability FAQ

Is the Mazda CX-9 reliable?

Above-average reliability. The Mazda CX-9 earns a B+ overall reliability grade from GradeMyCar, with a B- for its engine and a B+ for its transmission. It ranks 4th of 23 midsize SUVs we grade. The engine (B-) is the weaker of its two major components, so ask for oil-change records and have a mechanic check for leaks and oil consumption. Grades are based on 579 owner complaints filed with NHTSA for the 2013-2023 model years, adjusted for how many were sold.

How many miles will a Mazda CX-9 last?

A well-maintained Mazda CX-9 typically lasts around 170,000 miles, and owners have reported examples reaching 240,000 miles. About 8.6% reach 250,000 miles.

What are the best years for the Mazda CX-9?

Based on NHTSA owner complaints, the most reliable Mazda CX-9 model years are 2022 and 2023.

What Mazda CX-9 years should I avoid?

Based on NHTSA owner complaints, the Mazda CX-9 years to be most careful with are 2010 and 2018. The 2010 grades a D overall (engine C, transmission D). The 2018 grades a D overall (engine D, transmission C).

Is the Mazda CX-9 transmission reliable?

Is the Mazda CX-9 engine reliable?
