Kia Telluride reliability FAQ

Is the Kia Telluride reliable?

Poor reliability. The Kia Telluride earns an F overall reliability grade from GradeMyCar, with a D for its engine and a D for its transmission. It ranks 22nd of 23 midsize SUVs we grade. Both its engine (D) and transmission (D) grade poorly, so a pre-purchase inspection by a mechanic is a must. Grades are based on 1,665 owner complaints filed with NHTSA for the 2020-2023 model years, adjusted for how many were sold.

How many miles will a Kia Telluride last?

A well-maintained Kia Telluride typically lasts around 170,000 miles, and owners have reported examples reaching 240,000 miles. About 8.7% reach 250,000 miles.

What are the best years for the Kia Telluride?

No Kia Telluride model year stands out as exceptionally reliable in NHTSA owner complaints. The best-graded years are 2021, 2022 and 2023.

What Kia Telluride years should I avoid?

Based on NHTSA owner complaints, the Kia Telluride years to be most careful with are 2020 and 2021. The 2020 grades an F overall (engine F, transmission D), and its top engine/transmission problem is oil burning / oil dilution. The 2021 grades a D overall (engine C-, transmission C), and its top engine/transmission problem is engine failure / knocking.

Is the Kia Telluride transmission reliable?

GradeMyCar grades the Kia Telluride transmission a D (poor reliability). Reliability varies by model year, so grade the specific year you're looking at.

Is the Kia Telluride engine reliable?
