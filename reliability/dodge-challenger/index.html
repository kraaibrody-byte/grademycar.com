Dodge Challenger reliability FAQ

Is the Dodge Challenger reliable?

Excellent reliability. The Dodge Challenger earns an A- overall reliability grade from GradeMyCar, with an A- for its engine and an A- for its transmission. It ranks 1st of 5 sports cars we grade. Its engine (A-) and transmission (A-) grade about the same, so neither stands out as a weak point. Grades are based on 805 owner complaints filed with NHTSA for the 2013-2023 model years, adjusted for how many were sold.

How many miles will a Dodge Challenger last?

A well-maintained Dodge Challenger typically lasts around 170,000 miles, and owners have reported examples reaching 240,000 miles. About 8.5% reach 250,000 miles.

What are the best years for the Dodge Challenger?

Based on NHTSA owner complaints, the most reliable Dodge Challenger model years are 2008, 2017, 2018, 2020, 2021, 2022 and 2023.

What Dodge Challenger years should I avoid?

Based on NHTSA owner complaints, the Dodge Challenger years to be most careful with are 2009 and 2010. The 2009 grades a D overall (engine C-, transmission C-), and its top engine/transmission problem is stalling / loss of power. The 2010 grades a D overall (engine D, transmission C-), and its top engine/transmission problem is stalling / loss of power.

Is the Dodge Challenger transmission reliable?

GradeMyCar grades the Dodge Challenger transmission an A- (excellent reliability). Reliability varies by model year, so grade the specific year you're looking at.

Is the Dodge Challenger engine reliable?

GradeMyCar grades the Dodge Challenger engine an A- (excellent reliability).
