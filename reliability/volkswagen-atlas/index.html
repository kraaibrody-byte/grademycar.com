Volkswagen Atlas reliability FAQ

Is the Volkswagen Atlas reliable?

Below-average reliability. The Volkswagen Atlas earns a C overall reliability grade from GradeMyCar, with a C- for its engine and a B for its transmission. It ranks 15th of 23 midsize SUVs we grade. The engine (C-) is the weaker of its two major components, so ask for oil-change records and have a mechanic check for leaks and oil consumption. Grades are based on 1,765 owner complaints filed with NHTSA for the 2018-2023 model years, adjusted for how many were sold.

How many miles will a Volkswagen Atlas last?

A well-maintained Volkswagen Atlas typically lasts around 150,000 miles, and owners have reported examples reaching 210,000 miles. About 7.2% reach 250,000 miles.

What are the best years for the Volkswagen Atlas?

Based on NHTSA owner complaints, the most reliable Volkswagen Atlas model years are 2020.

What Volkswagen Atlas years should I avoid?

Based on NHTSA owner complaints, the Volkswagen Atlas years to be most careful with are 2019 and 2021. The 2019 grades a D overall (engine C-, transmission C), and its top engine/transmission problem is stalling / loss of power, which a recall covers. The 2021 grades a D overall (engine D, transmission C+), and its top engine/transmission problem is stalling / loss of power, which a recall covers.

Is the Volkswagen Atlas transmission reliable?

GradeMyCar grades the Volkswagen Atlas transmission a B (above-average reliability). Reliability varies by model year, so grade the specific year you're looking at.

Is the Volkswagen Atlas engine reliable?

GradeMyCar grades the Volkswagen Atlas engine a C- (below-average reliability).

Grades are estimates for a make and model across model years, not a specific vehicle. Always get a pre-purchase inspection and check open recalls by VIN.
