Volkswagen Passat reliability FAQ

Is the Volkswagen Passat reliable?

Excellent reliability. The Volkswagen Passat earns an A- overall reliability grade from GradeMyCar, with a B for its engine and an A for its transmission. It ranks 2nd of 11 midsize cars we grade. The engine (B) is the weaker of its two major components, so ask for oil-change records and have a mechanic check for leaks and oil consumption. Grades are based on 1,889 owner complaints filed with NHTSA for the 2013-2022 model years, adjusted for how many were sold.

How many miles will a Volkswagen Passat last?

A well-maintained Volkswagen Passat typically lasts around 180,000 miles, and owners have reported examples reaching 210,000 miles. About 7.3% reach 250,000 miles.

What are the best years for the Volkswagen Passat?

Based on NHTSA owner complaints, the most reliable Volkswagen Passat model years are 2011, 2017, 2018, 2019 and 2020.

What Volkswagen Passat years should I avoid?

Based on NHTSA owner complaints, the Volkswagen Passat years to be most careful with are 2006. The 2006 grades an F overall (engine D, transmission D), and its most common complaint is airbags, which a recall covers.

Is the Volkswagen Passat transmission reliable?

GradeMyCar grades the Volkswagen Passat transmission an A (excellent reliability). Reliability varies by model year, so grade the specific year you're looking at.

Is the Volkswagen Passat engine reliable?

GradeMyCar grades the Volkswagen Passat engine a B (above-average reliability).

Grades are estimates for a make and model across model years, not a specific vehicle. Always get a pre-purchase inspection and check open recalls by VIN.
