Subaru WRX reliability FAQ

Is the Subaru WRX reliable?

Above-average reliability. The Subaru WRX earns a B overall reliability grade from GradeMyCar, with a B for its engine and a B for its transmission. It ranks 4th of 5 sports cars we grade. Its engine (B) and transmission (B) grade about the same, so neither stands out as a weak point. Grades are based on 643 owner complaints filed with NHTSA for the 2013-2023 model years, adjusted for how many were sold.

How many miles will a Subaru WRX last?

A well-maintained Subaru WRX typically lasts around 150,000 miles, and owners have reported examples reaching 210,000 miles. About 7.2% reach 250,000 miles.

What are the best years for the Subaru WRX?

Based on NHTSA owner complaints, the most reliable Subaru WRX model years are 2005, 2019, 2020 and 2023.

What Subaru WRX years should I avoid?

Based on NHTSA owner complaints, the Subaru WRX years to be most careful with are 2012 and 2013. The 2012 grades a D overall (engine D, transmission D), and its most common complaint is airbags, which a recall covers. The 2013 grades a D overall (engine D, transmission C-), and its most common complaint is airbags, which a recall covers.
