Kia Soul reliability FAQ

Is the Kia Soul reliable?

Average reliability. The Kia Soul earns a C+ overall reliability grade from GradeMyCar, with an F for its engine and a B- for its transmission. It ranks 6th of 10 subcompact SUVs we grade. The engine (F) is the weaker of its two major components, so ask for oil-change records and have a mechanic check for leaks and oil consumption. Grades are based on 4,681 owner complaints filed with NHTSA for the 2013-2023 model years, adjusted for how many were sold.

How many miles will a Kia Soul last?

A well-maintained Kia Soul typically lasts around 185,000 miles, and owners have reported examples reaching 240,000 miles. About 8.5% reach 250,000 miles.

What are the best years for the Kia Soul?

No Kia Soul model year stands out as exceptionally reliable in NHTSA owner complaints. The best-graded years are 2010, 2011 and 2019.

What Kia Soul years should I avoid?

Based on NHTSA owner complaints, the Kia Soul years to be most careful with are 2014, 2015 and 2020. The 2020 grades an F overall (engine F, transmission D), and its most common complaint is stalling / loss of power, which a recall covers. The 2014 grades a D overall (engine F, transmission C), and its most common complaint is engine failure / knocking.

Is the Kia Soul transmission reliable?

GradeMyCar grades the Kia Soul transmission a B- (average reliability). Reliability varies by model year, so grade the specific year you're looking at.

Is the Kia Soul engine reliable?

GradeMyCar grades the Kia Soul engine an F (poor reliability).

Grades are estimates for a make and model across model years, not a specific vehicle. Always get a pre-purchase inspection and check open recalls by VIN.
