Ford Bronco reliability FAQ

Is the Ford Bronco reliable?

Poor reliability. The Ford Bronco earns a D overall reliability grade from GradeMyCar, with a D for its engine and a C for its transmission. It ranks 3rd of 4 off-road SUVs we grade. The engine (D) is the weaker of its two major components, so ask for oil-change records and have a mechanic check for leaks and oil consumption. Grades are based on 835 owner complaints filed with NHTSA for the 2021-2023 model years, adjusted for how many were sold.

How many miles will a Ford Bronco last?

A well-maintained Ford Bronco typically lasts around 200,000 miles, and owners have reported examples reaching 300,000 miles. About 10.8% reach 250,000 miles.

What are the best years for the Ford Bronco?

No Ford Bronco model year stands out as exceptionally reliable in NHTSA owner complaints. The best-graded years are 2021, 2022 and 2023.

What Ford Bronco years should I avoid?

Based on NHTSA owner complaints, the Ford Bronco years to be most careful with are 2022. The 2022 grades an F overall (engine D, transmission C-).

Is the Ford Bronco transmission reliable?

GradeMyCar grades the Ford Bronco transmission a C (below-average reliability). Reliability varies by model year, so grade the specific year you're looking at.

Is the Ford Bronco engine reliable?

GradeMyCar grades the Ford Bronco engine a D (poor reliability).
