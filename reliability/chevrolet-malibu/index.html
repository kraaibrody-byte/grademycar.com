Chevrolet Malibu reliability FAQ

Is the Chevrolet Malibu reliable?

Average reliability. The Chevrolet Malibu earns a C+ overall reliability grade from GradeMyCar, with a C+ for its engine and a C for its transmission. It ranks 8th of 11 midsize cars we grade. Its engine (C+) and transmission (C) grade about the same, so neither stands out as a weak point. Grades are based on 4,499 owner complaints filed with NHTSA for the 2013-2023 model years, adjusted for how many were sold.

How many miles will a Chevrolet Malibu last?

A well-maintained Chevrolet Malibu typically lasts around 180,000 miles, and owners have reported examples reaching 230,000 miles. About 8.1% reach 250,000 miles.

What are the best years for the Chevrolet Malibu?

No Chevrolet Malibu model year stands out as exceptionally reliable in NHTSA owner complaints. The best-graded years are 2014, 2015 and 2019.

What Chevrolet Malibu years should I avoid?

Based on NHTSA owner complaints, the Chevrolet Malibu years to be most careful with are 2009, 2010 and 2018. The 2018 grades an F overall (engine D, transmission D). The 2009 grades a D overall (engine C, transmission D).

Is the Chevrolet Malibu transmission reliable?

GradeMyCar grades the Chevrolet Malibu transmission a C (below-average reliability). Reliability varies by model year, so grade the specific year you're looking at.

Is the Chevrolet Malibu engine reliable?

GradeMyCar grades the Chevrolet Malibu engine a C+ (average reliability).
