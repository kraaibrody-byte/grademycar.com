Is the Chrysler Pacifica reliable?

How many miles will a Chrysler Pacifica last?

A well-maintained Chrysler Pacifica typically lasts around 150,000 miles, and owners have reported examples reaching 210,000 miles. About 7.4% reach 250,000 miles.

What are the best years for the Chrysler Pacifica?

No Chrysler Pacifica model year stands out as exceptionally reliable in NHTSA owner complaints. The best-graded years are 2019, 2020 and 2023.

What Chrysler Pacifica years should I avoid?

Based on NHTSA owner complaints, the Chrysler Pacifica years to be most careful with are 2017, 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022 and 2023. The 2017 grades an F overall (engine F, transmission F). The 2018 grades an F overall (engine F, transmission F).
