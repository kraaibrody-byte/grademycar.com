Is the Nissan Xterra reliable?

How many miles will a Nissan Xterra last?

We estimate a well-maintained Nissan Xterra lasts around 190,000 miles. That's a rough figure for the model across all years, not measured for each model year, so check the grade for the year you're looking at.

What are the best years for the Nissan Xterra?

Based on NHTSA owner complaints, the most reliable Nissan Xterra model years are 2001, 2003, 2004, 2011, 2014 and 2015.

What Nissan Xterra years should I avoid?

Based on NHTSA owner complaints, the Nissan Xterra years to be most careful with are 2005, 2006 and 2007. The 2005 grades an F overall (engine F, transmission F), and its top engine/transmission problem is transmission slipping / rough shifting. The 2006 grades an F overall (engine F, transmission F), and its top engine/transmission problem is transmission slipping / rough shifting.
