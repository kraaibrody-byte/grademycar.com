Nissan Murano reliability FAQ

Is the Nissan Murano reliable?

Excellent reliability. The Nissan Murano earns an A overall reliability grade from GradeMyCar, with an A for its engine and an A for its transmission. It ranks 1st of 23 midsize SUVs we grade. Its engine (A) and transmission (A) grade about the same, so neither stands out as a weak point. Grades are based on 767 owner complaints filed with NHTSA for the 2013-2023 model years, adjusted for how many were sold.

How many miles will a Nissan Murano last?

A well-maintained Nissan Murano typically lasts around 160,000 miles, and owners have reported examples reaching 220,000 miles. About 7.9% reach 250,000 miles.

What are the best years for the Nissan Murano?

Based on NHTSA owner complaints, the most reliable Nissan Murano model years are 2013, 2016, 2017, 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021 and 2023.

What Nissan Murano years should I avoid?

Based on NHTSA owner complaints, the Nissan Murano years to be most careful with are 2009. The 2009 grades a D overall (engine C-, transmission D), and its most common complaint is brake problems, which a recall covers.

Is the Nissan Murano transmission reliable?

GradeMyCar grades the Nissan Murano transmission an A (excellent reliability). Reliability varies by model year, so grade the specific year you're looking at.

Is the Nissan Murano engine reliable?

GradeMyCar grades the Nissan Murano engine an A (excellent reliability).
