Kia Sportage reliability FAQ

Is the Kia Sportage reliable?

Above-average reliability. The Kia Sportage earns a B overall reliability grade from GradeMyCar, with a C- for its engine and a B+ for its transmission. It ranks 5th of 13 compact SUVs we grade. The engine (C-) is the weaker of its two major components, so ask for oil-change records and have a mechanic check for leaks and oil consumption. Grades are based on 1,983 owner complaints filed with NHTSA for the 2013-2023 model years, adjusted for how many were sold.

How many miles will a Kia Sportage last?

A well-maintained Kia Sportage typically lasts around 185,000 miles, and owners have reported examples reaching 240,000 miles. About 8.4% reach 250,000 miles.

What are the best years for the Kia Sportage?

Based on NHTSA owner complaints, the most reliable Kia Sportage model years are 2005, 2006, 2007, 2008, 2009, 2010 and 2019.

What Kia Sportage years should I avoid?

Based on NHTSA owner complaints, the Kia Sportage years to be most careful with are 2023. The 2023 grades an F overall (engine D, transmission D), and its top engine/transmission problem is stalling / loss of power, which a recall covers.

Is the Kia Sportage transmission reliable?

GradeMyCar grades the Kia Sportage transmission a B+ (above-average reliability). Reliability varies by model year, so grade the specific year you're looking at.

Is the Kia Sportage engine reliable?

GradeMyCar grades the Kia Sportage engine a C- (below-average reliability).

Grades are estimates for a make and model across model years, not a specific vehicle. Always get a pre-purchase inspection and check open recalls by VIN.
