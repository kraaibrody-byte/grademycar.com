Is the Toyota Corolla reliable?

How many miles will a Toyota Corolla last?

A well-maintained Toyota Corolla typically lasts around 220,000 miles, and owners have reported examples reaching 350,000 miles. About 10.1% reach 250,000 miles.

What are the best years for the Toyota Corolla?

Based on NHTSA owner complaints, the most reliable Toyota Corolla model years are 2008, 2011, 2012, 2013, 2014, 2015, 2016, 2017, 2018, 2019 and 2023.
