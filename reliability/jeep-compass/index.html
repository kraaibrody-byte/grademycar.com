Jeep Compass reliability FAQ

Is the Jeep Compass reliable?

Average reliability. The Jeep Compass earns a C+ overall reliability grade from GradeMyCar, with a C+ for its engine and a C+ for its transmission. It ranks 6th of 10 subcompact SUVs we grade. Its engine (C+) and transmission (C+) grade about the same, so neither stands out as a weak point. How many miles will a Jeep Compass last?

A well-maintained Jeep Compass typically lasts around 150,000 miles, and owners have reported examples reaching 210,000 miles. About 7.2% reach 250,000 miles.

What are the best years for the Jeep Compass?

No Jeep Compass model year stands out as exceptionally reliable in NHTSA owner complaints. The best-graded years are 2015, 2021 and 2023.

What Jeep Compass years should I avoid?

Based on NHTSA owner complaints, the Jeep Compass years to be most careful with are 2007, 2008, 2011, 2014 and 2018. The 2007 grades an F overall (engine D, transmission D), and its top engine/transmission problem is stalling / loss of power. The 2018 grades an F overall (engine F, transmission D), and its top engine/transmission problem is stalling / loss of power, which a recall covers.
