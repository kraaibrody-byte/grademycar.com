Ford Expedition reliability FAQ

Is the Ford Expedition reliable?

Poor reliability. The Ford Expedition earns a D overall reliability grade from GradeMyCar, with a C for its engine and a D for its transmission. It ranks 6th of 6 full-size SUVs we grade. The transmission (D) is the weaker of its two major components, so ask for transmission service records and pay close attention to shifting on a test drive. Grades are based on 2,618 owner complaints filed with NHTSA for the 2013-2023 model years, adjusted for how many were sold.

How many miles will a Ford Expedition last?

A well-maintained Ford Expedition typically lasts around 210,000 miles, and owners have reported examples reaching 300,000 miles. About 13.1% reach 250,000 miles.

What are the best years for the Ford Expedition?

Based on NHTSA owner complaints, the most reliable Ford Expedition model years are 2009.

What Ford Expedition years should I avoid?

Based on NHTSA owner complaints, the Ford Expedition years to be most careful with are 2005, 2018, 2019, 2020 and 2021. The 2018 grades an F overall (engine D, transmission F), and its most common complaint is transmission slipping / rough shifting, which a recall covers. The 2019 grades an F overall (engine F, transmission F), and its most common complaint is transmission slipping / rough shifting, which a recall covers.

Is the Ford Expedition engine reliable?
