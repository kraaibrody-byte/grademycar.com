Questions

Good questions before you sign up.

Where do the grades come from?

From the complaints owners file with NHTSA, the federal agency that tracks vehicle defects and runs recalls. We've sorted more than 800,000 of them by problem and compared each model year with other cars from the same year, adjusted for how many were sold.

Is the report a diagnosis?

No, and it says so. It shows what's common on that model year and at what mileage. Your inspection and your note tell the customer what's going on with their car. The report shows the pattern behind it.

How does texting work?

On your phone, "Text it" opens your messages app with the customer's number and the link filled in, and it sends from your own number. On a computer, copy the link and send it however you like. We don't charge for texts.

Will my customer see a letter grade?

Only on pre-purchase inspections, where someone is deciding whether to buy. For repairs and service, the customer already owns the car, so the report leaves out the A–F grade and focuses on recalls, what owners of that model report, and what to watch for.

Does my customer need anything?

No. The link opens a normal web page on any phone. No app, no account, no email.

How does the free trial work?

You get 14 days free. You add a card at sign-up and aren't charged until the trial ends. Cancel anytime from Shop & billing, and you won't be charged.
